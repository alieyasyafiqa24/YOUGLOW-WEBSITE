How To Treat Eczema ?

Eczema is a skin condition characterized by inflammation, redness, and itching. It can lead to the development of dry, scaly patches on the skin. Consequences of eczema may include discomfort, irritation, and a negative impact on the skin's appearance. Eczema is often linked to factors like genetics, environmental triggers, and immune system responses. Managing eczema involves moisturizing, avoiding triggers, and, in some cases, using prescribed medications to alleviate symptoms.
